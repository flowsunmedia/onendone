Open the scorecard ★Tap this. It saves itself on your phone. Download the fileKeeps a copy in your Files / Downloads

iPhone

  1. Tap Open the scorecard.
  2. Tap the share button ⎋ → Add to Home Screen. Now it's an app icon.

Or tap Download the file, then open it from the Files app.

Android

  1. Tap Open the scorecard.
  2. Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Or tap Download the file and open it from Downloads.

How to use it

  1. Pick your starting hole.
  2. Type each player's name.
  3. Tap scores as you go. Hole by hole is easiest on a phone.
  4. When your group finishes, tap ★ Finalize, take a screenshot, and hand it in with your paper card.

Keep your paper card too — the admin checks both.
